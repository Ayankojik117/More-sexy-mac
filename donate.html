Where it goes

Your donation, at work

MaC Foundation is run entirely by volunteers, so contributions go directly toward the cost of running our projects on the ground.

  • Materials and supplies for community outreach and welfare drives
  • Equipment for sports, wellness, and mentorship sessions
  • Plantation drives and environmental clean-up initiatives
  • Workshop costs for women empowerment and skill-building programs
  • Learning materials and resources for our education initiatives

Prefer to ask first? Reach out to our team before contributing, or write to us directly at mac.makeachangefoundation@gmail.com.
